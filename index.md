# Privacy Policy

**Peter Test — Petrix AI WhatsApp test project**  
Last updated: 24 November 2026

## About this project

Peter Test is a personal development project used to test a Petrix AI chatbot through WhatsApp. This notice explains how information is handled when you use its messaging, feedback, support and workflow features. The developer can be contacted at Njuguna.lydia73[@gmail.com](mailto:peternjosh7365@gmail.com).

## Information processed

Depending on what you share and which features you use, the project processes:

* Your WhatsApp phone number or business-scoped user identifier, and any profile name supplied with your messages.
* The messages, survey answers, feedback and attachments you send.
* Support-ticket details and contact information you provide when requesting assistance.
* Technical conversation information, including message identifiers, timestamps, processing errors and delivery status.

Please use fictional information when testing and avoid sending passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary sensitive documents.

## How information is used

Information is used to receive and respond to messages, test chatbot workflows, generate replies when AI features are enabled, collect feedback, and provide requested support. Conversation information may also be reviewed to diagnose failures, improve the test experience and prevent abuse.

The project developer and authorised support users may review conversations and related records for these purposes.

## Storage and service providers

WhatsApp messages are transmitted through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Petrix stores conversation history and related test records in the application's configured storage.

When AI features are enabled, relevant messages and context may be sent to the AI service configured for the project. When a workflow sends an email, creates a support record or calls a connected service, relevant information may be provided to that service or the intended recipient to perform the requested task.

Meta, hosting, AI and other connected service providers may process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Processing locations depend on the services configured for the project. Contact the developer if you would like details of the services being used for your test.

We do not sell personal information or use WhatsApp conversation content for unrelated advertising.

## Retention

Test records are retained only for as long as needed to operate the project, investigate technical issues, provide requested support and meet applicable obligations. Records that are no longer needed will be deleted or anonymised.

The developer handles requests to delete information held by the project. Deleting a conversation in your own WhatsApp application does not automatically delete records already received by this project.

## Data deletion

To request deletion of personal information held by this project, email Njuguna.lydia73[@gmail.com](mailto:peternjosh7365@gmail.com) with the subject **Peter Test — Data Deletion Request**.

Include the WhatsApp number or user identifier used for the test and, if possible, the approximate date of your conversation. Do not include passwords or sensitive documents. Limited additional information may be requested to confirm that the request relates to your own records.

After verifying the request, the developer will delete or anonymise the personal information held by this project, except where retention is required by applicable obligations. Requests concerning information held independently by Meta or another provider must be directed to that provider.

You can also use this contact address to ask questions about information held by the project or request correction of information you provided.

## Security

The project uses application access controls to restrict access to authorised users and HTTPS for its publicly exposed WhatsApp webhook. Please limit test submissions to information needed for the feature you are testing.
